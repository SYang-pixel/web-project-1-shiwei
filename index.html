Space and Galaxies

Explore the space and galaxies with this website!


The space and galaxies are very interesting and mysterious. There are many things we haven't discovered about the space and galaxies yet. The space is a massive place that exists above the Earth's atmosphere, and it contains countless stars, planets, asteroids, and comets.

Galaxies are massive systems of stars, dusts, and dark matters that are controlled by gravity. They come in different/various shapes and sizes from galaxies like Milky Way to other interesting galaxies. Studying Space and Galaxies helps us learn more about the universe, and the potential of finding other planets similar to Earth.

The current galaxy we are in right now is called the Milky way. The Earth and our solar system are located in the Onion Arm (also called the Orion Spur or Local Arm).

The current solar system we are in is called the "Solar System". That is the official name of our solar system.



  • The Milky Way grew to its current size by swallowing up smaller galaxies over billions of years.
  • Our Solar System is constantly traveling at roughly 828,000 km/h (about 514,000 mph) relative to the center of the Milky Way galaxy.
  • Even at this extreme speed, the Milky Way is so massive that it still takes our Solar System 225-250 million Earth years to complete one full orbit around the galactic center.
  • Scientists estimate that there are two trillion galaxies in the observable universe, each containing billions or trillions of stars.
  • IC 1101 is considered the largest known galaxy by the extent of its stellar body and mass.
  • There are hundreds of known moons in our solar system. With the most amount of moon in one planet being Saturn.
  • Saturn has nearly 300 confirmed moons (including around 293 total recognized moons).
